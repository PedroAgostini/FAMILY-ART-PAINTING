Questions Homeowners Ask

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We visit your home, look at the job with you and send a written estimate by text or email. There is no obligation.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Family Art Painting is licensed and carries liability insurance. Ask us for a copy during your home visit.

Do you paint in the winter?

Yes. The cold months are a great time for interior painting, cabinet painting, drywall repair and post-construction cleaning. Exterior painting and power washing are scheduled for the warmer season.

Who will actually be working in my home?

Our family. Leonardo and Josilaine run every project, so you deal with the owners from the first call to the last coat.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Marlborough, MA and work in towns across Massachusetts. Call or text to check your town.

Is there anything you do not do?

We do not offer epoxy floors or wallpaper installation. We do remove old wallpaper and get the walls ready for paint.
